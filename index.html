Creator FAQ

Can we use suction camera rigs and rolling shot mounts? ▼

Yes! All our machines have paint-protection film (PPF) installed. Rubber suction mounts (Tilta, DJI, GoPro) and rolling shot camera car setups are 100% permitted.

How does the 15% "Tag & Save" Creator Subsidy work? ▼

Simply check the "Tag & Save 15%" box in our calculator and provide your Instagram or YouTube handle. When you post your reel/video, tag @ignite.wheels. The 15% discount is applied immediately to your booking invoice!

What documents are required for booking? ▼

Valid Original Driving License (min. 2 years old for self-drive) and Government Photo ID (Aadhaar or Passport). Verification is completed digitally within 15 minutes.

Which shoot locations and hubs do you cover? ▼

We deliver on flatbeds directly to Noida Film City, Aerocity Delhi, Buddh International Circuit (BIC), Yamuna Expressway, Gurgaon Cyber City, and private film studios.
